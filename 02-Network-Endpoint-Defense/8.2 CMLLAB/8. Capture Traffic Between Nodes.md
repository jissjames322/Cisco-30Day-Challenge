## Scenario

Cisco Modeling Labs is a great tool for exploring network technologies. **Whenever you dive into network operations, you may have to capture and analyze traffic exchanged between the nodes.** You will use the **packet capture and analysis feature to inspect ICMP and Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) packets on a link.**
