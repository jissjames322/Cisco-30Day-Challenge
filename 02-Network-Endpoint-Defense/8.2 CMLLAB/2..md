
Cisco Modeling Labs is a platform that provides a realistic experience working with virtual networks without the obstacles that can appear in "real life." To get the most out of Cisco Modeling Labs, it is important to understand its functions and capabilities.

In this course, you will dive into the basics of using Cisco Modeling Labs and gain an understanding of where it is and how to properly use it.

This course is divided into four main activities, each giving you the fundamental knowledge needed and preparing you for more complex activities within Cisco Modeling Labs.

# Create the First Lab Topology

With Cisco Modeling Labs, each network topology that you create is considered a Lab. Labs can range from a simple topology containing a single network device to full replications of real-world networks with multiple sites, WANs, and hosts containing numerous nodes.

You will start by opening a new workbench page, changing its title and adding a brief description.

1. Login to Cisco MLLABS 
2. Dashboard Page
3. Locate the **ADD** and **IMPORT** buttons in the upper-right corner. Click **ADD** to open a new workbench page.
4. You will be presented with an empty workbench page, where all aspects of the lab are managed.

The Workbench page is used to create and modify a lab's topology and to interact with the lab simulation. It contains a graphical canvas where you can create, modify, and interact with the lab topology. The canvas is permanently displayed.

There is also a sidebar pane that provides a view of the different details and actions for the selected node. If no node is selected, then the sidebar is hidden. If you select a node or a link in the canvas, the tabs and details in the sidebar will show information about the selected topology node.

5. Explore the **NODES**, **PANES** and **GUIDE** panes on the right side of the toolbar.
6. To change your lab title and add a description, click the drop-down field located in the middle of the page, above the toolbar.
7. Change the **Lab Title** to: My First Lab Topology. Change the **Lab Description** to: Network Simulation with Cisco Modeling Labs.
8. Continue exploring around your workbench. Notice how your lab title has changed.
9. When done, return to the dashboard page by clicking **DASHBOARD** in the menu bar, or by clicking the **Cisco Modeling Labs** logo at the top-left corner of the page.
10. Check that **My First Lab Topology** workbench has been added to your dashboard page.
